01 / The challenge

Why parent training needs practice beyond the clinic

The project explores how immersive simulations and adaptive AI feedback could help parents rehearse responses in challenging situations.

⌁

Childhood aggression

Persistent aggressive behavior can be associated with later behavioral, academic, and emotional difficulties.

↗

Generalization gap

Skills practiced in calm clinical settings may be difficult to transfer to stressful home environments.

◎

Engagement

Traditional role-play and homework can be challenging to sustain, creating an opportunity for interactive practice.

02 / System workflow

From interaction to adaptive feedback

A four-stage conceptual pipeline connects immersive input with language understanding and scenario response.

STEP 01

Immersive input

VR audio and gestures are captured as interaction input, with speech recognition where available.

STEP 02

Zero-shot classification

BART Large MNLI maps parent utterances to candidate behavioral labels.

STEP 03

Knowledge retrieval

Vector search and retrieval-augmented generation provide contextual guidance from the project knowledge base.

STEP 04

Adaptive feedback

The virtual child response changes according to the interpreted parent interaction.

03 / VR interaction

Practice in a safe, responsive environment

The proposed experience uses familiar home situations to create repeatable opportunities for parent practice.

  • ✓Ecological validity
    Simulated home environments aim to make practice more contextually realistic.
  • ✓Adaptive difficulty
    Virtual child behavior can escalate or de-escalate in response to parent actions.
  • ✓Safe sandbox
    Scenarios include tantrums, non-compliance, sibling conflict, and verbal provocation.

04 / AI methodology

A layered language understanding stack

Each model supports a different part of the interpretation and feedback pipeline.

facebook/bart-large-mnli

BART Large MNLI — natural language inference for zero-shot classification of parent utterances into candidate labels, without requiring a task-specific labelled training set.

all-MiniLM-L6-v2

Sentence Transformer — semantic similarity checks for concepts such as praise, encouragement, and behavior redirection.

DistilBERT

Sentiment analysis — sentiment signals and conditional consequence checks to support safer, more pedagogically appropriate feedback.

06 / Responsible design

Ethics, privacy, and limitations

Responsible use matters.

The project specification highlights biometric and voice privacy, demographic bias mitigation, and informed consent. It also identifies the need for rigorous clinical trials and longitudinal evaluation to determine whether skills practiced in VR transfer to home settings. This prototype should not be presented as a validated treatment or a replacement for professional care.
